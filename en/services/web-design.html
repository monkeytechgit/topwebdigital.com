FAQ

Questions about website design

How long does it take?

A brochure site with up to 5 pages is ready in 7 to 10 business days. Larger sites or online stores take 12 to 15 days, counted from when we receive your content.

Do I need my own copy and photos?

It helps, but it's not required. We can write SEO-friendly copy and use high-quality stock images, or coordinate a photo shoot.

What if I don't like the design?

You get unlimited revisions during the design stage. If the first concept doesn't work for you, we refund your deposit.
