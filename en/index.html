    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    How much does a website cost?

    Professional sites start at $690 (one-time) and include domain, one year of hosting and basic SEO. Online stores start at $1,990. Use the quote tool for an exact range.

    How does payment work?

    50% deposit to start and 50% when you approve the site, before it goes live. We accept cards, ACH and Zelle. Split it into 3 payments if you prefer.

    Do you send invoices?

    Yes, you get a proper invoice for every payment.

    Are there costs after year one?

    Only domain and hosting renewal, which we tell you up front. If you're on a care plan, it's included.

    How long does it take?

    7 to 15 business days depending on size. A site with up to 5 pages is usually ready in 7 to 10 days.

    What do I need to get started?

    Your logo (we can design one), copy or information about your business, photos and social links. We send a simple questionnaire to gather everything.
